Delete your account

You can delete your CoLift account and data at any time, right from the app.

In the app

  • Open CoLift and go to the You tab.
  • Tap the Settings gear (top right).
  • Scroll to Delete account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

What gets removed

Deleting your account permanently erases your profile, workouts and history, moments, comments, reactions, friend connections, group memberships, RSVPs, join requests and your group chat messages, and removes your uploaded photos, videos and profile picture from storage. It also revokes the Sign in with Apple link. Deletion happens when you confirm and cannot be undone. Copies in encrypted provider backups may remain for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

Groups you own

If you own a group, ownership passes to its longest-standing admin, or if there is none its longest-standing member, along with the events you hosted. If nobody else is in the group, it is deleted together with its events, chat messages, posts and requests. You can also delete the group yourself in the app before you delete your account.

Strava and Apple Health

Connecting Strava is not part of this version, so there is no Strava grant to remove. Anything you shared to Strava yourself stays there until you delete it in Strava. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health under your control.

CoLift+ subscribers

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription. Cancel CoLift+ first in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to stop future charges.

Need help?

If you can't access the app, email colift.app.official@gmail.com from the email on your account and we'll delete your account and data for you.
