Questions

What is COLIFT?

A workout log for lifting with a friend, even when you cannot go at the same time. A friend leaves you their workout, you run it when you can, and at the exercises they shared you see their photo and how long ago they left it. Then you leave one back.

How does my friend show up at my set?

When you run a friend's workout, the photo they took at an exercise appears at that same exercise, with how long ago they left it. Only friends who run that lift see it, and nothing is public. You can report or block anyone, any time.

Who is COLIFT for?

Anyone who wants to lift with a friend whose hours do not match theirs: a gym friend on a different schedule, a friend who moved away after graduating, or someone who misses having a team to lift with. It is built in Ontario, Canada. You start with one friend; groups come after.

Do we have to work out at the same time or place?

No, that's the point. Catch a friend's lift live, or run their exact workout later, at a different gym and a different time. The recap shows how far apart you lifted, like “6h apart”.

Is there any competition or ranking?

No. There are no rankings, no leaderboards and no wagers. Friends see moments, and the Our week page counts what you did together. Your own history and personal records stay yours.

Is it free?

Yes. The core (replays, the feed and reactions) is free. COLIFT+ is an optional upgrade: Coach Maya, our automated planner, runs your split, your whole story is kept up to 500 lifts (free keeps your latest 7), and you get one rest-day cover a month. Cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Can I find lifting groups near me?

Start with one friend; groups and events come after. Finding and joining public groups is for people 18 and older, and location is optional: you can pick a city instead. People 13 to 17 can use private groups with friends. See the Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy.

Is my data private?

You choose who sees each post: Everyone, Friends or Only me. Your profile photo is stored at a public link, as the privacy policy explains. We don't sell your data or run ads. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel COLIFT+ or delete my account?

Cancel COLIFT+ anytime in iOS Settings. You can delete your account right in the app. Here's how.
