Questions

What is COLIFT?

A social fitness app built on two things: replays and us. A friend leaves you their workout, you run it on your time, and every lift lands as one shared post you both live on.

What are "both your reactions"?

When a workout posts, you each drop a reaction selfie: a real photo of you, not just an emoji. Both reactions sit on the post together, so it's a shared moment, not a like count.

Do we have to work out at the same time or place?

No, that's the point. Catch a friend's lift live, or replay their exact workout later, at a different gym and a different time. The post shows both clocks, so 6am and 7pm still land as one session.

Is there any competition or ranking?

No. COLIFT rewards showing up, not your weights or PRs. No leaderboards, no ranking. The win is the habit, together. We call it presence over performance.

Is it free?

Yes. The core (replays, the feed, reactions, presence, streaks) is free forever. COLIFT+ is an optional upgrade: your day's lift picked from real people, matched to your goals, and everything you two build, kept forever. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Can I find lifting groups near me?

Yes. CoLift has groups and events. Finding and joining public groups is for people 18 and older, and location is optional: you can pick a city instead. People 13 to 17 can use private groups with friends. See the Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy.

Is my data private?

You control who sees your workouts (friends you connect with). Your profile photo is stored at a public link, as the privacy policy explains. We don't sell your data or run ads. See our Privacy Policy.
