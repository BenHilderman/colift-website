Before your first session

Good to
know.

What can I use today?

The current App Store app lets you discover community workouts, create and log your own sessions, save workouts, and share a photo and reaction on the workout you completed. The screenshots on this page preview the next update’s creator-media panels, sample experience and album composer. They include clearly labelled sample content.

Do I need a friend to start?

No. You can create your own workout and train solo. Sign in to discover available community workouts and connect with other people. The upcoming update also puts a no-account sample workout up front, so you can try the replay experience first.

Do we need to train at the same time?

No. Follow the workout when it fits your schedule. Choose your own weights, rest and pace. Creator photos or videos appear when they’ve actually been attached to an exercise; a finish-only post does not provide a demonstration for every movement.

Will my replay become a shared post?

Today, you can add your photo and reaction to the workout post you trained from. The upcoming replay-post flow also gives your result its own post with a source credit, so people can trace the workout back to its creator. That new flow is still being prepared for release.

Can I import an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube workout?

Video imports are coming soon and are not currently enabled. The planned CoLift+ allowance is 5 imports per month, with up to 8 processing attempts and no rollover. Supported formats and clips will need a plan review before you train. Imports and new subscription purchases remain off for now.

Is CoLift free?

The core workouts, workout logging and friend features are free. CoLift+ is an optional upgrade for additional progress and recap features; new subscription purchases are not currently enabled. Video imports are a planned CoLift+ feature. Existing subscribers can manage their subscription in Apple Account settings.

Do I have to post photos or videos?

No. Finishing saves your workout privately. Posting is a separate choice. You choose the audience for shared content; a public post can be visible beyond your friends. The upcoming album lets you select which of your captures to include. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Where can I get help?

Visit Support for account, workout and safety help. You can report content or block people from the app. CoLift does not replace qualified exercise instruction; choose movements and loads appropriate for you.
