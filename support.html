Support

We're a small team and we read everything.

Get in touch

Email us at colift.app.official@gmail.com for bugs, feedback, account questions, anything. We aim to reply within a couple of days. Reports of a child at risk, self-harm or a threat are different: when you file one in the app it alerts us immediately and we look at it straight away. If you email about something urgent or unsafe, put "Safety" in the subject. If you are in danger, contact emergency services first.

Common questions

How do I add a friend?

Open CoLift, tap to invite, and share your link. Once they accept, you'll see each other's presence and feed.

How do I manage or cancel CoLift+?

CoLift+ is billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future charges; your current period stays active until it ends.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and data directly in the app. Step-by-step: Delete account.

How do I report a group, event, message or person?

For a post, open it and tap the three dots (More options). For a group or event, use the three-dot menu on its page and choose Report. For a message, tap or long-press the bubble and choose Message options. For a member, tap their name. You can Report and Block a person from the post, message and member menus. Reports are saved and reviewed by a person. Urgent reports (a child at risk, self-harm, a threat) alert us immediately and are looked at straight away; other reports are reviewed within a couple of days. For anything urgent or unsafe, you can also email colift.app.official@gmail.com with "Safety" in the subject. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services first. See the Community Guidelines.

Where are the crisis lines?

Open the You tab, then Settings, then Safety and help, and tap Need help now?. In a group, the menu item Need help? opens the same page.

Why can't I see Explore or Local groups?

Finding and joining public groups is for people 18 and older, and needs you to confirm your age in the app. People 13 to 17 can join private groups with an invite code or link. If your age band was entered by mistake, email us from your account email; we may ask for proof before changing it.

Why can't I find someone by their @handle?

Adults can find other adults, and people 13 to 17 can find other people 13 to 17 by exact @handle. You need to have answered the age question first. Someone from the other age group, or who has not answered it yet, will show as not found; that is on purpose. A friend link works between people in the same age group: a link between an adult and a person under 18 is refused.

How do I join a group from a link or code?

Open CoLift, go to Groups and tap Join with an invite code, then paste the code or the whole colift.fit/g/ link.

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

A workout is started on the Watch: open CoLift on your Watch and tap Start workout. If you start a workout on your iPhone, its exercises are sent to the Watch, where you still tap Start workout. Heart rate and energy stay on your devices; calories can appear on a recap card only if you choose to share that card.

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can pick a city instead. If you tap Use my location, CoLift reads one approximate position and rounds it on your phone; the rounded cell stays on your phone and is never sent as a precise position. You can turn location off in iOS Settings at any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is my data private?

Yes. You control who sees your workouts (friends you connect with), group content is visible to the members of that group, and your profile photo is stored at a public link, as described in the privacy policy. We never sell your data or run ads. See the Privacy Policy.
