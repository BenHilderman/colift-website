Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 2026

CoLift is about showing up for a friend, not flexing, not performing. These rules keep it that way. By using CoLift you agree to follow them. Breaking them gets content removed and can get your account suspended or removed.

Be supportive

Encourage, don't tear down. Reactions and comments should lift people up. Names, @handles, captions, comments, exercise notes, group names and descriptions, and chat messages are checked automatically and known abusive words and phrases are rejected. The check cannot catch everything, so if something slips through, report it.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or hate based on who someone is.
  • Nudity, sexual content, or graphic violence.
  • Impersonation or pretending to be someone you're not.
  • Spam, scams, or promoting dangerous behavior (including disordered-eating or extreme-cut content).
  • Anything illegal.

These rules cover everything you post, including the short notes and photos you add to an exercise of a workout. Friends who run your workout see them at that exercise, so the same rules apply as for messages: no contact details or links, no selling, no sexual content, no harassment, and nothing involving a minor at risk. People under 18 are covered by the same rules and the under-13 rule is unchanged: CoLift is for people 13 and over.

In groups and events

Groups and events are for 18 and older when they are public or request-to-join. People 13 to 17 can use private, invite-only groups. In groups, events and group chat these are also not allowed:

  • Selling, ads, promoting a business or product, or posting links to bring people elsewhere.
  • Sexual content, flirting that is unwanted, or using a group to look for hookups.
  • Contacting or targeting minors, or any content that puts a minor at risk.
  • Offering or promoting supplements, steroids or drugs.
  • Sharing phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses, yours or anyone's. Phone numbers, emails, links and @handles are blocked for your safety in group chat, shared workout cards, event plans, group and event names and descriptions, display names, join-request notes, exercise notes, exercise names, photo captions, post captions and lift titles. Keep the conversation in CoLift.
  • Dangerous challenges or pressure to lift past your limits.
  • Fake events, spam groups, or names that pretend to be CoLift or someone else.

Meeting up safely

  • Meet in a public place such as a gym, park or track. Never use a private home as the venue.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • You do not owe anyone a reply, your number, or your location. CoLift never shows your location to other people.
  • Public events need a public place, not a home address, and must start between 8 AM and 8:59 PM local time. Friend connections between adults and people under 18 are not available: no such link is ever created.
  • You can leave a group, block a person or report anything at any time. If you feel unsafe, leave and contact local emergency services if needed.
  • CoLift does not vet members or venues. Use your own judgement.

If you run a group

Owners and admins set the tone. You can approve or decline join requests, remove or ban members, mute a member, lock the chat, rotate the invite code, delete a message and cancel an event. Use them quickly when someone breaks these rules and report serious cases to us.

Keep it real, keep it kind

Share your own workouts and moments. Don't post other people without their okay. No body-shaming, yours or anyone's. CoLift has no leaderboards on purpose; the win is showing up.

Report & block

See something off? Open the post and tap its three-dot menu (More options), or use the three-dot menu on a group, an event, a group message (Message options) or a member, to report it or block the person. To report a photo or a note someone left on a workout you are running, tap the three dots on it, or report the post it came from. Blocking takes effect immediately and you can undo it in You, Settings, Blocked people. Every report is saved and sent to us for human review, and we remove violating content and suspend or remove offending accounts. You can say why: a child may be at risk, self-harm or suicide, a threat or violence, sexual content, hate, harassment, spam or a scam, pretending to be someone, or something else.

Urgent reports. Reports about a child at risk, self-harm or a threat are urgent. They send us an immediate alert and are looked at straight away, ahead of everything else. If someone is in danger, call emergency services first; CoLift is not an emergency service. Other reports are reviewed within a couple of days.

Automatic hiding is a precaution, not a verdict. To slow harm while we look, some things are hidden automatically. When several different established accounts (at least a week old, with real activity in the app) report the same group, event or message, it is hidden while we review it, and it is restored if we find no violation. A group started by the CoLift team is never hidden this way. A single report from an established member that a group message puts a child at risk, or is sexual in a group that includes anyone under 18, hides that message from the other members at once (its sender still sees it), whoever wrote it, and the person who wrote it is told. It never hides the whole group. Only an ordinary member can be paused for 24 hours, and only when the report meets our checks; group owners and admins are never paused, and the CoLift team's own group is never hidden or paused this way. A pause stays in place if the person leaves the group and joins again. We review these reports and undo the hiding and the pause if we find no violation. A note that qualifies under the same checks as a message is hidden from other people while we review it (the note, not the whole workout), and the person who wrote it sees that it is under review. A photo or video that is reported, or that an automatic check flags, is not shown to others while it is held, and the person who shared it sees that it is under review. Reported and held photos and videos are kept as evidence, not deleted straight away, even if the account that posted them is deleted (see the Privacy Policy for how long). If we hid something by mistake, email us with the name of the group or the photo and we will restore it. We act on reports promptly. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

If you or someone else needs help

In an emergency, call 911. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Canada and the US). In Canada, Kids Help Phone is open to young people at 1-800-668-6868 or by texting 686868. Outside Canada and the US, call your local emergency number or see findahelpline.com. CoLift is not a crisis service and cannot respond in real time, so please use these first.

These resources are always in the app: open the You tab, then Settings, then Safety and help, and tap Need help now? (the crisis lines are one tap deeper than the safety tips). Inside a group, the menu item Need help? opens the same page, and it opens after you report something urgent.
