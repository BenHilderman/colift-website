Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 2026

CoLift is about showing up for a friend, not flexing, not performing. These rules keep it that way. By using CoLift you agree to follow them. Breaking them gets content removed and can get your account suspended or removed.

Be supportive

Encourage, don't tear down. Reactions and comments should lift people up. Names, @handles, captions, comments, group names and descriptions, and chat messages are checked automatically and abusive wording is rejected. Anything that slips through, report it.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or hate based on who someone is.
  • Nudity, sexual content, or graphic violence.
  • Impersonation or pretending to be someone you're not.
  • Spam, scams, or promoting dangerous behavior (including disordered-eating or extreme-cut content).
  • Anything illegal.

In groups and events

Groups and events are for 18 and older when they are public or request-to-join. People 13 to 17 can use private, invite-only groups. In groups, events and group chat these are also not allowed:

  • Selling, ads, promoting a business or product, or posting links to bring people elsewhere.
  • Sexual content, flirting that is unwanted, or using a group to look for hookups.
  • Contacting or targeting minors, or any content that puts a minor at risk.
  • Offering or promoting supplements, steroids or drugs.
  • Sharing phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses, yours or anyone's. Group chat blocks phone numbers, emails, links and @handles for your safety. Keep the conversation in CoLift.
  • Dangerous challenges or pressure to lift past your limits.
  • Fake events, spam groups, or names that pretend to be CoLift or someone else.

Meeting up safely

  • Meet in a public place such as a gym, park or track. Never use a private home as the venue.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • You do not owe anyone a reply, your number, or your location. CoLift never shows your location to other people.
  • Public events need a public place, not a home address, and are not allowed late at night (10 pm to 6 am local time). Friend connections between adults and people under 18 are not available.
  • You can leave a group, block a person or report anything at any time. If you feel unsafe, leave and contact local emergency services if needed.
  • CoLift does not vet members or venues. Use your own judgement.

If you run a group

Owners and admins set the tone. You can approve or decline join requests, remove or ban members, mute a member, lock the chat, rotate the invite code, delete a message and cancel an event. Use them quickly when someone breaks these rules and report serious cases to us.

Keep it real, keep it kind

Share your own workouts and moments. Don't post other people without their okay. No body-shaming, yours or anyone's. CoLift has no leaderboards on purpose; the win is showing up.

Report & block

See something off? Tap the menu on any post, comment, direct send, group, event or group message, or open a person's profile, to report it or block them. Blocking takes effect immediately and you can undo it in You, Settings, Blocked people. Every report is saved and sent to us for human review, and we remove violating content and suspend or remove offending accounts. You can say why: a child may be at risk, self-harm or suicide, a threat or violence, sexual content, hate, harassment, spam or a scam, pretending to be someone, or something else. Reports about a child at risk, self-harm or a threat are handled first. When several different established members report the same group, event or message, it is hidden while we review it, and it is restored if we find no violation. We act on reports promptly. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

If you or someone else needs help

In an emergency, call 911. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Canada and the US). In Canada, Kids Help Phone is open to young people at 1-800-668-6868 or by texting 686868. CoLift is not a crisis service and cannot respond in real time, so please use these first.
