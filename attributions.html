Map and place data

  • OpenStreetMap: map drawing and the list of gyms, fitness and sports centres, climbing gyms, pools, studios, parks and tracks in place search are © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0. Our place list is a derived database of OpenStreetMap data. If you would like a copy of the extract, email us. Venues are mapped by volunteers, so some gyms may be missing or out of date.
  • GeoNames: city names, regions, coordinates and population come from GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. We filtered and reformatted the data.
  • Protomaps: map tiles are packaged as a single PMTiles archive in the Protomaps basemap layout, built from OpenStreetMap data. See protomaps.com. The PMTiles reader library is BSD-3-Clause, copyright Brandon Liu.
  • MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1 draws the map. BSD-3-Clause, copyright MapLibre contributors. See maplibre.org.
  • Noto Sans map label fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
