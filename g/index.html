Get CoLift
  1. Get CoLift on the App Store and sign in with Apple.
  2. Confirm your age in the app.
  3. Open this link again, and CoLift takes you to the group. If it does not open by itself, go to Groups, tap Have an invite code?, then paste this code.

This link does not contain a valid invite code. Ask the person who invited you to send it again.

CoLift is a 13+ app; groups are 18+. Finding and joining public groups is for people 18 and older. People aged 13 to 17 can join only private, invite-only groups from people they know. Meet in a public place and tell a friend where you are going. CoLift does not vet members or venues. Read the Community Guidelines.

This page does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and the invite code stays in your browser.
